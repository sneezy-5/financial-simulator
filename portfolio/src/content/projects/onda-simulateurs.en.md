## The idea

Make concrete the calculations most people are subjected to without
understanding them: the real cost of a loan, what the lines of a payslip hide,
tax by regime, the point at which a business becomes profitable.

## The tools

- **Bank loan simulator** — monthly payment, total cost, interest share, nominal
  rate vs APR, borrowing capacity and debt ratio, assignable share (UEMOA /
  BCEAO rules), amortization schedule, borrower profile scoring.
- **Payslip simulator** — gross → taxable → net breakdown, CNPS/CNSS
  contributions, income tax, per country.
- **SME tax comparator** — estimate of corporate tax and help choosing a regime
  (flat-rate / actual).
- **Profitability tools** — selling price, gross margin and margin rate,
  break-even point.

Everything is free, no account, usable from the browser.

## Role & stack

Designed the flows and the **calculation engines** — the core of the work: tax
and banking rules translated into testable code — with a **Vue 3 / Vite**
interface, installable as a PWA.

## Outcome

Educational tools that also serve as an entry point into the ONDA ecosystem:
understand first, then equip yourself.
